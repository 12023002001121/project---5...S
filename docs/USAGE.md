## How to interact with the dApp

1. **Connect Wallet:**
   Click "Connect Wallet" on the top right. We recommend using Albedo for a seamless experience on desktop and mobile, or Freighter if you have the extension installed.
2. **Obtain Testnet XLM:**
   Make sure you are on the Stellar Testnet. You can use the official Stellar laboratory to fund your account.
3. **Voting on Proposals:**
   - Browse the active proposals on the dashboard.
   - Click "Vote YES" or "Vote NO". 
   - Note: Your voting weight is determined by your Reputation (REP) balance at the time the proposal was created.
   - For every vote you cast, you earn +1 REP.
4. **Creating a Proposal:**
   - Navigate to the "New Proposal" tab.
   - Fill in the required fields (Title, Description, Requested Amount).
   - Ensure you have sufficient XLM to cover the network fees.
   - Submit the proposal.
5. **Depositing to Treasury:**
   - Go to the Treasury section.
   - Enter the amount of XLM you wish to deposit.
   - Confirm the transaction.

## Error Handling
If you encounter errors like `Transaction Rejected` or `Already Voted`, the UI will provide a helpful error message to guide you. If you get a `Stale Cache` error, use the "Clear Cache & Reload" button.
